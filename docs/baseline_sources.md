# Baseline Code Sources for SV-VideoRAG

## 1. LLoVi
Paper: A Simple LLM Framework for Long-Range Video Question-Answering  
Venue: EMNLP 2024  
Code: https://github.com/CeeZh/LLoVi  
Role in our experiments: Caption aggregation / caption-RAG baseline.

## 2. DrVideo
Paper: DrVideo: Document Retrieval Based Long Video Understanding  
Venue: CVPR 2025  
Code: https://github.com/Upper9527/DrVideo  
Role in our experiments: Long-video-as-document retrieval baseline.

## 3. Video-RAG
Paper: Video-RAG: Visually-aligned Retrieval-Augmented Long Video Comprehension  
Venue: NeurIPS 2025  
Code: https://github.com/Leon1207/Video-RAG-master  
Role in our experiments: Visually-aligned auxiliary text RAG baseline.

## 4. VideoTree
Paper: VideoTree: Adaptive Tree-based Video Representation for LLM Reasoning on Long Videos  
Venue: CVPR 2025  
Code: https://github.com/Ziyang412/VideoTree  
Role in our experiments: Query-adaptive evidence selection baseline.

## Our method
SV-VideoRAG: Semantic Verification Enhanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Long Video Reasoning.
